The Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In) under the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY) issued its comprehensive cybersecurity review.

## Threat Incidents in the Indian Cyber Sphere
- **Total Reported Incidents:** CERT-In tracked and mitigated 1,391,340 incidents across Indian government, public infrastructure, and consumer sectors.
- **Phishing Distribution:** 31.4% of incidents involved unauthorized domain lookalikes, fraudulent netbanking portals, or impersonation of Indian statutory bodies.

## Regional Attack Vectors
1. **Smishing / SMS Header Forgery:** Attackers utilize international SIM gateways or spoofed alphanumeric headers to deliver urgent banking KYC cancellation warnings.
2. **Malicious APK Sideloading:** Scammers urge victims to install fake banking utilities or 'PM Scheme' apps outside the Google Play Store, extracting SMS OTPs to drain bank accounts.

## National Directives
Citizens must report cyber incidents within 24 hours to the 1930 National Cybercrime Reporting Portal to trigger inter-bank financial freezes.
