The Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C), established under the Ministry of Home Affairs, released this review of citizen-facing digital fraud.

## The 'Digital Arrest' Phenomenon
International syndicates operating across Southeast Asia execute coordinated extortion schemes:
1. The victim receives a robocall or text claiming a parcel sent via FedEx or DHL contained passports, synthetic narcotics, or forged bank cards.
2. The caller connects the victim via video call (Skype, WhatsApp) to an actor wearing counterfeit police or customs uniforms with simulated police station backdrops.
3. The victim is coerced into staying on video call for hours or days ('digital arrest') while liquidating life savings into 'Supreme Court clearance accounts'.

## Citizen Protection Directives
Police officers and judicial authorities NEVER conduct arrests, trials, or asset verification over video calls or messaging apps.
