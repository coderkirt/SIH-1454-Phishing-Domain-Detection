The FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3) compiled reports from 880,418 complaints filed during the recent reporting cycle.

## Victim Counts and Loss Statistics
- **Most Prevalent Crime Type:** Phishing / Spoofing remained the number one reported crime type by a wide margin, representing 298,878 individual complaints.
- **Top Financial Losses:**
  - Investment Fraud: $4.57 billion (surpassing all other categories).
  - Business Email Compromise: $2.90 billion across 21,489 complaints.
  - Tech Support Scams: $924 million.

## Mechanics of Cryptocurrency Pig Butchering
Syndicates cultivate trust over months via WhatsApp, Telegram, or dating apps before directing victims to fraudulent decentralized finance (DeFi) liquidity mining or lookalike trading apps. Once deposited, victim funds are laundered through cross-chain bridges and privacy mixers.

## Public Safety Advisories
Never send wire payments or cryptocurrency based solely on unverified phone calls, SMS prompts, or romantic online connections.
