The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) published its annual findings on banking fraud and digital payment security.

## Statistical Analysis of Banking Fraud
- **Total Fraud Losses:** Scheduled Commercial Banks reported 36,075 crore INR in total fraud losses during the fiscal year.
- **Digital Hegemony:** Frauds involving cards, internet banking, and digital wallets accounted for 70.5% of all reported incidents by count.

## Modus Operandi
- Fraudsters contact customers posing as bank representatives, warning of blocked accounts or unpaid utility bills.
- Victims are directed to unverified third-party websites where they input card details and OTPs.

## Regulatory Directives for Banks
Banks must enforce velocity limits on newly added payees, mandatory cooldown periods after credential changes, and instant two-factor confirmation for international e-commerce.
