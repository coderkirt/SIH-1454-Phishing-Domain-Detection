Proofpoint's annual State of the Phish benchmark surveys 7,500 employees and 1,050 IT security professionals across 15 countries.

## Phishing Prevalence & Impact
- **Breach Rates:** 71% of surveyed organizations experienced at least one successful phishing compromise resulting in credential loss, financial theft, or data exfiltration.
- **Telephone-Oriented Attack Delivery (TOAD):** Scammers increasingly send emails without links, prompting targets to call a fraudulent call center where agents talk victims into installing remote desktop software.

## Psychological Triggers
Lures capitalizing on fear of negative consequences (payroll errors, tax audits, immediate job termination) produced the highest employee click-through rates.

## Mitigation Strategy
User education must emphasize conversational skepticism and verify communication authenticity outside of the delivery channel.
