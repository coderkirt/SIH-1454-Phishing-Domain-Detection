Naukri.com is India's premier employment portal. Job fraud syndicates impersonate Naukri recruiters to exploit vulnerable job seekers.

## Official Web Infrastructure
- Candidate and recruiter portals reside on `naukri.com` and `naukrigulf.com`.

## Common Scam Operations
- Fake Placement Guarantee: Scammers contact candidates via WhatsApp claiming they have been selected for multinational roles, demanding a 'gate pass' or 'document verification fee'.
- Unsolicited Offer Letters: Counterfeit job letters bearing forged corporate logos and requesting refundable equipment deposits.

## Security Inviolates
- Naukri.com and legitimate employers NEVER charge registration fees, interview deposits, or laptop security fees.
- Candidates should never transfer funds to personal UPI handles or savings accounts for job placements.
